Connect your iPhone

  1. Install MTGLogger through SideStore and open it.
  2. Join the same Wi-Fi network as your server.
  3. In Settings, enter your MTGLogger address. For this server: http://192.168.1.254:5173.
  4. Tap Connect, allow camera and local-network access, then open Scan.
The server must be running and reachable from your phone. Your phone stores pending captures until the server confirms them. Release details and verification status
